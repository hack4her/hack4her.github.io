# Sponsor Hack4Her

Hack4Her is on a mission to bridge the gender gap in technology and innovation and you can be part of it to! By sponsoring Hack4Her, you're not just supporting an event; you're investing in the future of tech, empowering women, and promoting diversity and inclusion within the industry. And what is a better place to invest in tech than in the world-class top technology region, located here in Eindhoven.

## Why Sponsor Hack4Her?

- **Engage with Talent**: Connect with a pool of talented, motivated individuals passionate about technology and innovation.
- **Impact the Tech Community**: Your support helps us provide a platform for aspiring women in tech to learn, network, and showcase their talents.
- **Brand Visibility**: Align your brand with innovation, diversity, and empowerment, enhancing your corporate social responsibility profile.

## Sponsor opportunities
Hack4Her is an event organised in Amsterdam and Eindhoven. The last event in Amsterdam had over 230 applications of which 150 were selected to participate in the 3-day event. For the upcoming event in Eindhoven we expect the same amount of students. During the event the main event is the hackathon. Here students get to take on a challenge which is provided by an organisation, resembling a problem similar to a real-world issue the organisation may be facing. Next to the hackathon there are multiple other activities such as workshops, keynotes and a network event. 

By sponsoring Hack4Her you get the opportunity to organise one of the previously mentioned activites and engaging with the specialists of the future. Furthermore there is also the opportunity to provide merchandise, send representatives to recruit or provide any other way of financial support. We'd love to tailor a sponsorship experience that aligns with your goals.
